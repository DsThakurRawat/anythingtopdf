# Anything To PDF

A document conversion and PDF text editing tool that converts multiple media formats into PDFs and allows precise text editing and search-and-replace on existing PDF documents.

## Capabilities

- **Convert Images to PDF**: Converts single or multiple images (PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP) into high-quality PDF documents.
- **Convert Presentations to PDF**: Converts PowerPoint presentations (PPT, PPTX) directly to PDF with layout preservation.
- **Extract & Merge ZIP Archives**: Safely unpacks multi-file ZIP archives (such as downloaded photo sets or WhatsApp exports), sorts files in natural numerical order, and merges them into a single PDF.
- **Search & Replace Text in PDFs**: Finds and replaces target words, phrases, dates, names, or numbers across all pages of a PDF document.
- **Sentence & Word Spacing Reflow**: Dynamically recalculates word spacing and shifts surrounding words when replacing text of different lengths to eliminate overlapping text or broken layouts.
- **Automatic Typography Matching**: Detects and preserves original font family (Base-14 Helvetica, Times, Courier, etc.), font weight (Bold, Italic), exact font size, baseline alignment, and text color.
- **Text Redaction & Removal**: Securely removes/redacts unwanted or sensitive text from PDF pages without leaving ghost vector artifacts.
- **Case Preservation**: Intelligently matches and preserves the case formatting (ALL CAPS, Title Case, lowercase) of replaced text.
